Grilled Cheese Sandwich

Prep time : 5 mins

Extra cheesy grilled cheese sandwich

Yield : 4 sandwiches

Ingredients





Steps

  1. Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  3. Place 4 slices of bread, buttered side down, on the skillet.
  4. Add a slice of cheddar and mozzarella cheese on each slice of bread.
  5. Top with the remaining slices of bread, buttered side up.
  6. Cook until the bread is golden brown and the cheese is melted, about 3-4 minutes per side.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot.
