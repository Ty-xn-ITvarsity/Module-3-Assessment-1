Vegetable Curry

Prep time : 30 mins

Yield : 4 servings

Ingredients









Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onion and cook until soft.
  2. Add garlic, curry powder, and cumin. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add mixed vegetables and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Pour in coconut milk, season with salt and pepper, and bring to a simmer.
  5. Cover and cook for 15–20 minutes, until vegetables are tender.
  6. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve hot with rice or naan.
