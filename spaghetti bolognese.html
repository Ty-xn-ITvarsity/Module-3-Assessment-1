Spaghetti Bolognese

Prep time : 15 mins

Cook time : 30 mins

Yield : 4 servings

Ingredients










Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions; drain.
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat.
  3. Add onion and garlic; sauté until translucent.
  4. Increase heat to medium-high; add minced beef.
  5. Cook until browned, breaking up the meat with a spoon.
  6. Stir in chopped tomatoes, tomato paste, and oregano.
  7. Season with salt and pepper; simmer for 10 minutes.
  8. Serve sauce over spaghetti; garnish with Parmesan cheese.
