Lemonade

Prep time :10 mins

Yield :4

Ingredients





Steps

  1. In a small saucepan, combine 1 cup water and 1 cup sugar. Heat and stir until sugar dissolves to make a simple syrup. Let cool.
  2. In a pitcher, combine lemon juice, simple syrup, and remaining 3 cups cold water. Stir well.
  3. Add ice cubes and garnish with lemon slices if desired. Serve chilled.
