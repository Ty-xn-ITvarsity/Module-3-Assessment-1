Vanilla Pudding

Prep time : 10 mins

Yield : 4 servings

Ingredients






Steps

  1. In a saucepan, whisk together sugar, cornstarch, and salt. Gradually add milk, whisking until smooth.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until mixture thickens and starts to boil.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in butter and vanilla extract.
  4. Pour pudding into serving dishes. Chill for at least 2 hours before serving.
