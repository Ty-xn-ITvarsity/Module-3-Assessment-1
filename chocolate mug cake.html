Chocolate Mug Cake

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 2 minutes

Yield: 1 mug cake

Ingredients









Instructions

  1. In a microwave-safe mug, whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Add milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract. Mix until smooth and well combined.
  3. Stir in chocolate chips if using.
  4. Microwave on high for 1 minute and 30 seconds to 2 minutes, until the cake has risen and is set in the center.
  5. Let cool for a minute before enjoying straight from the mug!
